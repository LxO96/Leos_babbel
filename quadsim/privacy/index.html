Quadsim privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

In short: The app collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app has no access to the internet. Your progress and settings stay on your device. Google Play gives the developer limited statistics, described below.

This policy covers the Quadsim app for Android, developed by Leo (“the developer”). It explains how the app handles information on your device.

What the app keeps on your device

Quadsim saves a few things in its own storage on your phone or tablet, so you can carry on where you left off:

  • Your progress through the lessons: which you have passed, your stars and your best times.
  • Your settings, such as the stick mode, your game controller's button layout, the sound and the music.
  • The missions you plan, and the results of survey flights.

None of it is about you as a person, and none of it ever leaves your device.

No internet access

Quadsim does not ask Android for permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere. The music credits in Settings link to the music's pages on Pixabay: tapping one opens it in your web browser, and from then on that website's own privacy policy applies.

Game controllers

If you fly with a game controller, the app reads its sticks and buttons only to fly the drone. The only thing it keeps is the button layout you set up, with your other settings on your device.

Information from Google Play

Like every app on Google Play, Quadsim is distributed by Google, which shares some information with the developer through the Google Play Console. This information comes from Google, not from the app:

  • Statistics: aggregated numbers such as installs, uninstalls, ratings, countries and device models. These don't identify you.
  • Crash and performance reports: if you allow your device to share usage and diagnostics with Google, reports of crashes and freezes include technical details such as the device model and Android version.

The developer uses this information only to fix problems and answer support requests. Google's collection of it is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Sharing with others

The app sends no data to the developer or to any third party. The information the developer receives through Google Play is never sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Everything Quadsim keeps is on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes all of it. No data is stored on any server, so the developer holds no data to delete on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
