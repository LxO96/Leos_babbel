Wigglecam privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

In short: The app collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app. What you capture stays on your phone unless you share it yourself. Google Play gives the developer limited statistics and purchase records, described below.

This policy covers the Wigglecam app for Android, developed by Leo (“the developer”). It explains how the app handles information on your device.

Photos and captures

Wigglecam uses your camera to take the frames that make up each wigglegram. Those frames, and your edits to them, are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere.

When you export a wigglegram (as an MP4, GIF, WebP, or DNG frames), the file is saved to your phone's gallery. It leaves your phone only if you share it, for example through Android's share sheet, and then the app you share it with handles it under its own policy.

Location

Saving location is optional and off until you turn it on, either when the app first asks or later in Settings. If you turn it on:

  • Wigglecam reads your location only while its camera screen is open, never in the background.
  • The location is saved with each capture, so your gallery can show it on a map.
  • Exported MP4, WebP and DNG files include it, so anyone you send them to can see where they were taken. GIFs never include it.

You can turn it off at any time in Wigglecam's Settings, or take the permission away in Android's settings.

Purchases

Wigglecam Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play, and payment details are never shared with the developer. The app only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Pro, so it can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. Google's handling of the purchase is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Information from Google Play

Like every app on Google Play, Wigglecam is distributed by Google, which shares some information with the developer through the Google Play Console. This information comes from Google, not from the app:

  • Statistics: aggregated numbers such as installs, uninstalls, ratings, countries and device models. These don't identify you.
  • Crash and performance reports: if you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google, reports of crashes and freezes include technical details such as the device model and Android version. They never include your photos or location.
  • Purchase records: for purchases of Pro, the order number, date, price and country. The developer can also look up an order by the buyer's email address.

The developer uses this information only to fix problems, answer support requests, handle refunds and keep accounts. Google's collection of it is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take the frames.
  • Location (optional): to tag captures with where they were taken, as described above.

Sharing with others

The app sends no data to the developer or to any third party. The information the developer receives through Google Play is never sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Everything Wigglecam keeps is on your phone. Delete a capture in the app's Library, or uninstall Wigglecam to remove all of them. Exported files stay in your gallery until you delete them there. No data is stored on any server, so the developer holds no data to delete on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
