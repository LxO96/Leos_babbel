Wigglecam privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

In short: Wigglecam collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you capture stays on your phone unless you share it yourself.

This policy covers the Wigglecam app for Android, made by Leo ("I"). It explains what the app does with information on your phone.

Photos and captures

Wigglecam uses your camera to take the frames that make up each wigglegram. Those frames, and your edits to them, are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere.

When you export a wigglegram (as an MP4, GIF, WebP, or DNG frames), the file is saved to your phone's gallery. It leaves your phone only if you share it, for example through Android's share sheet, and then the app you share it with handles it under its own policy.

Location

Saving location is optional and off until you turn it on, either when the app first asks or later in Settings. If you turn it on:

  • Wigglecam reads your location only while its camera screen is open, never in the background.
  • The location is saved with each capture, so your gallery can show it on a map.
  • Exported MP4, WebP and DNG files include it, so anyone you send them to can see where they were taken. GIFs never include it.

You can turn it off at any time in Wigglecam's Settings, or take the permission away in Android's settings.

Purchases

Wigglecam Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; I never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Pro, so it can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. Google's handling of the purchase is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take the frames.
  • Location (optional): to tag captures with where they were taken, as described above.
  • Vibration: for small haptic cues while capturing and editing.
  • Internet and Google Play billing: only to buy Pro and check that you own it. Nothing else goes over the network.

Sharing with others

I don't collect, sell or share any information about you, because the app sends none to me or to anyone else.

Deleting your data

Everything Wigglecam keeps is on your phone. Delete a capture in the app's Library, or uninstall Wigglecam to remove all of them. Exported files stay in your gallery until you delete them there. Since nothing is stored on a server, there's nothing for me to delete on your behalf.

Children

Wigglecam is not directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
